# Decisions

## Purpose
To record important architectural, design, and implementation decisions made by the AI or user, serving as a lightweight Architecture Decision Record (ADR) system for memory context.

## Update Rules
- Append a new record whenever a significant choice is made that affects project architecture, dependencies, or conventions.
- Do not modify past decisions; if a decision is reversed, add a new entry referencing the old one.

## Template Structure
### Decision: [Title]
- **Date:** [Date of decision]
- **Context:** [What is the problem or situation?]
- **Decision:** [What was decided?]
- **Consequences:** [What is the impact of this decision?]
